# Offline world map

Made with Natural Earth. Land geometry: Natural Earth 1:110m land, public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Retrieved 2026-10-02. world_land.json retains polygon exterior rings, coordinates
rounded to 3 decimal places; no political borders are included.

Airport coordinates come from the existing local aviation database and retain its
own source attribution. Displayed arcs are spherical shortest-path references,
not actual routes, airways, navigation instructions, or live aircraft positions.
# Country boundaries

`world_countries.json` is generated by `scripts/prepare_map.py` from Natural Earth
1:50m admin-0 country polygons (242 features). Source SHA-256 is embedded in JSON.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ (public domain).
Boundaries are indicative cartographic data, including disputed areas, not a legal definition.

# Optional imagery and weather

Satellite tiles are loaded only after choosing Satellite. EOxCloudless 2025 by
EOX IT Services GmbH contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025.
https://maps.eox.at/ · https://cloudless.eox.at/
Imagery license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
Non-commercial usage; attribution appears in the UI. Imagery is not shipped in
the application. Only visible tiles are fetched; a bounded in-memory cache is used.
The service may rate-limit requests; the offline map remains available.

Wind forecasts: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs · https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
Free non-commercial endpoint, no key/account; data attribution CC BY 4.0.
Only coordinates and selected pressure level are requested when explicitly enabled.
Forecasts describe real-world weather and may differ from RFS.
