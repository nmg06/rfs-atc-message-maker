What this is
------------
Origin and destination per callsign, worked out from where aircraft were
actually observed departing and arriving — not a curated schedule. The
evidence columns are part of the answer: a row chosen from several
candidates on thin evidence is a weaker claim than one seen on many days,
and `confidence` is the measured hit rate for the bucket a row falls in.
Column meanings travel in manifest.json.
